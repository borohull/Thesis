(Section~\ref{sec:testing-system}), together with their category, expected
source document, and reference answer. Reference answers for in-scope questions
were written manually by consulting the source documents directly. Out-of-scope
questions have no reference answer.

    1 &
    What is a strong prerequisite? &
    In-scope &
    prerequisites &
    Strong prerequisites are prerequisites without which the follow-up subject cannot be taken in the next semester, and the follow-up subject can only be registered for after the prerequisite subject has been fulfilled. Neptun will automatically deregister you from the subject if you do not meet this prerequisite. \\
    \hline

    2 &
    What is a weak prerequisite? &
    In-scope &
    prerequisites &
    A weak prerequisite can be taken in the same semester as the follow-up subject, but you must complete it first before you pass the follow-up. If not completed, your grade in the follow-up subject will automatically be failed or unfulfilled even if you pass its exam. \\
    \hline

    3 &
    If I fail a weak prerequisite, does it count toward my subject registration limit? &
    In-scope &
    prerequisites &
    If you register for the follow-up subject but cannot pass it in a given semester due to a failed prerequisite, the follow-up subject will not be counted into the maximum 3 subject registrations per subject limit during your studies. \\
    \hline

    4 &
    What is a passive semester at ELTE? &
    In-scope &
    Academic Regulations &
    A passive semester is the semester in which the student announces that he/she wishes to interrupt his/her studies, or cancels active registration, or fails to register until the given deadline. Student status is terminated if the student fails to register for the next semester following two consecutive semesters of suspension. \\
    \hline

    5 &
    What does active semester mean at ELTE? &
    In-scope &
    Academic Regulations &
    An active semester is the semester in which the student registers to start or continue his/her studies and does not cancel it over the course of the semester. \\
    \hline

    6 &
    What financial support is available for ELTE students? &
    In-scope &
    Academic Regulations &
    Financial support takes the following forms: academic scholarship, National Higher Education Scholarship, scientific scholarship, scholarships for competitions and conferences, public service scholarship, sports scholarship, cultural scholarship, compensation for parallel studies, ERASMUS+ Start, ``Good student, good athlete'' scholarship, professional scholarship, arts scholarship, regular social grant, one-time social grant, Bursa Hungarica, normative grants for foreign students, basic financial support, financial support for traineeships, and doctorate bursary. \\
    \hline

    7 &
    What is the result of the final examination at ELTE? &
    In-scope &
    Academic Regulations &
    The result of the final examination is the average of the grades given for the elements of the final examination. Any deferring regulation in connection with the grading of the final examination is approved by the Faculty Council on the proposal of the person responsible for the degree course. \\
    \hline

    8 &
    What is the student card and how do I get one? &
    In-scope &
    Student Card &
    The student card is a plastic orange-brown card similar in size to a bank card. You are eligible if you are staying at ELTE longer than 12 months, your semester status is active, and your Education ID has appeared in Neptun. To apply, obtain a NEK document from a Kormanyablak office, upload it in Neptun, and register your application electronically in the Neptun system. \\
    \hline

    9 &
    What housing options are available for ELTE students? &
    In-scope &
    housing &
    The ELTE Dormitory Centre provides accommodation to hundreds of ELTE international students in two cities, Budapest and Szombathely. The dormitories offer high-quality and affordable places and services, with more than half completely rebuilt within recent years, and a vivid community life. \\
    \hline

    10 &
    What should I do in case of a medical emergency in Hungary? &
    In-scope &
    health-insurance &
    Important emergency numbers: general emergency 112, ambulance 104, fire 105, police 107. \\
    \hline

    11 &
    What is the Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship? &
    In-scope &
    stipendium &
    Stipendium Hungaricum is a scholarship programme for foreign students, founded by the Hungarian Government in 2013. The programme aims to promote cultural understanding and economic and cultural relations between Hungary and other countries. The Department of Erasmus+ and International Programmes is responsible for the implementation of the programme at ELTE. \\
    \hline

    12 &
    What are the rules for exam registration in Neptun? &
    In-scope &
    Exam period &
    You must first register for the subject in Neptun to be eligible for the exam. A maximum of 3 exam attempts per subject are allowed in the same exam period. You can register for an exam and cancel or change exam dates at least 24 hours before the exam starts. Absence without a valid reason is counted as an unsuccessful attempt. \\
    \hline

    13 &
    How many credits are needed to complete a BSc at ELTE? &
    In-scope &
    curriculum &
    In the BSc programme you have to complete 180 credits: compulsory subjects 127 credits, compulsory elective subjects 23 credits, elective subjects 10 credits, and thesis consultation 20 credits. \\
    \hline

    14 &
    Can a Stipendium Hungaricum student go on Erasmus Scholarship? &
    In-scope &
    info-for-outgoing- erasmus-students &
    Stipendium Hungaricum students on BSc and MSc level are not eligible for Erasmus scholarship. \\

    What health insurance are ELTE students entitled to? &
    In-scope &

Stipendium Hungaricum, Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship, and Scholarship Programme for Christian Young People holders are eligible for both a TAJ card (non-private) and supplementary private health insurance. \\
